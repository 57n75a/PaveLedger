## Try the workflow

1. Open the private prototype. Inspect King Street East, including its image, recorded coordinates and activity history.
2. Use Assignment to select a team and analyst. Saving creates an in-app notification. Preview the analyst role to inspect its permitted cases.
3. Add a note and advance through investigation and confirmation. For a King Street East case, select the active demo contract and confirm scope before preparing a draft notice.
4. Advance a repair to Awaiting verification, demonstrate a clear-pass reviewer confirmation, close it and reopen it. Review the retained activity and changed analytics.
5. Try the contractor and auditor role previews, team/member creation, search, stage filtering and CSV export.

The prototype persists a single demonstration workspace, checks role scope on the server and provides a controlled status workflow. It does not run live camera inference, ingest real fleet evidence, calculate geospatial contract coverage, send external contractor messages or implement municipal SSO and production multi-tenancy. All camera images, contractor identities, confidence scores and ticket coordinates are illustrative. Membership creation does not send invitations. The initial private audience has been retained.

## Commercial starting proposal

CAD 18,000 for a 90-day pilot with up to five vehicles and 100 managed lane-km; hardware and connectivity are separate. Proposed software tiers are CAD 2,500, 6,500 and 15,000 per month, with annual commitment and bounded capacity. These are proposals to validate with buyers and supplier quotations.

The core engineering rule is to preserve the distinction between detection, responsibility, completion claimed and repair verified. A camera observation does not establish legal liability; a missing detection in an unclear image does not prove repair.
